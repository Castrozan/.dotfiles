# Email to a person

**Reader:** someone triaging an inbox from the subject line alone.

**Budget:** one phone screen, under about 150 words; one topic per email.

**Shape:**

1. Subject: a keyword (ACTION, DECISION, INFO, REQUEST), the topic, and the deadline.
2. The ask or answer in the first line.
3. Two to five lines of context, numbered when the reader may reply point by point.
4. The next step and its date.

**Avoid:** a subject like "Question", the ask buried under history, several unrelated topics, attachments that hold the
only copy of the key fact.

## Example

```text
Subject: DECISION by Fri Nov 14: renew Datadog at 3-year or 1-year term

Hi Sam,

Can you pick the renewal term by Friday? I recommend the 3-year term.

1. 3-year: $41k per year, price locked, 18% cheaper than 1-year.
2. 1-year: $50k, keeps us free to move to Grafana Cloud (evaluating until March).

If you pick 1-year, I'll reopen this in February with the evaluation result.

Thanks,
Ana
```

**Sources:** [BLUF summary of Sehgal, HBR 2016](https://gist.github.com/skullvalanche/250f3dca2316ee77669fbe01f4c4ac2b).
