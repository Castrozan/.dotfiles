# Wellput instruction audit

Reviewer: Gilgamesh. Review date: 2026-10-18. This is an audit snapshot, not a replacement policy.

Scope: all 68 body sentences in the 14 sections of `skills/wellput/SKILL.md`, its routing description, linked policies, generated delivery, Stop checks, and evaluation contracts. Sentence IDs match the [saved sentence review](skill-review.md). Canonical source snapshot: [dotfiles 5abc37265](https://github.com/Castrozan/.dotfiles/tree/5abc372655a6c4f91e1a4adbe7e3a2ea11dfb7fc/agent-harness/agent-instructions). Exported package snapshot: [Wellput 80aeb063](https://github.com/Castrozan/wellput/tree/80aeb06381904ed2952372cab8cf7b831d4bf782).

## Verdict

There are meaningful duplication, ambiguity, precedence, and ownership problems. The largest demonstrated defect is the gap between requested output and unconditional mechanical defaults. Three exact requested forms are rejected; a natural six-item procedure is also rejected. A paragraph hidden in a table passes. This establishes limits of the current composed contract, not a model-adherence rate.

The strongest parts should survive refinement: decision-first answers, exact referents and technical names, preservation of decision-changing facts, concrete recovery, ordered prerequisites, and a bounded handoff. Wisdom is not inherently waste. The goal is to give each behavior one owner and a clear condition, not to remove every rationale or minimize word count at any cost.

## Recommended refinement sequence

First settle the product contract for requested formats, then align prompts, mechanical checks, and composed evaluations. Otherwise rewriting the prose can leave the demonstrated failures intact.

Next consolidate fidelity, terminology, and recovery around the behaviors that existing controls protect. Give representation selection one owner and an explicit task-fit check. Keep useful judgment, conditional exceptions, and positive output guidance; remove repeated inventories rather than all explanatory language.

Finally compare a small consolidated candidate against a minimal-entry candidate using the same cases, actual configuration, and human-readable outputs. The [candidate comparison](skill-audit/relationships.md#candidate-designs) explains why those alternatives should be compared before adopting a structural rewrite. No token target or quality improvement is asserted in advance.

## Limits and preservation

This is a static instruction and dependency audit plus seven executed hook probes. It is not a new model benchmark. Historical evaluations, retained controls, and source inspection are evidence about intent and previous measurements, not proof of the current prompt's adherence or of a proposed rewrite's benefit.

The original skill, generated public skill, configuration, hooks, README, and media remain unchanged. The sentence review remains the stable source quotation index. Findings identify decisions and dependencies to carry into a later implementation, including tests that intentionally pin the present headings and wording.
