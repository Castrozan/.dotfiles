# Whole context cohesion: sentence audit

Sentence IDs S16-S17. [Audit overview](../../skill-audit.md). [Full sentence review](../../skill-review.md#whole-context-cohesion).

## Section assessment

This section earns its place by addressing a distinct failure: treating the newest addition as the new center of the
whole answer. The two sentences can share one short paragraph because one explains the trap and the other supplies the
operation. Do not use this safeguard to discount a recent user correction or steering message; the user may
intentionally change the priority.

Most useful content: the integration operation in S17. Main repair: bound restructuring to the needed change and
preserve the specific recency trap. See F06 and F07.

## S16: Merge with S17

> Do not give a new finding, concept, or passage priority because it is recent.

**Assessment:** This is a justified negative rule: it names the recency-priority trap rather than issuing generic
caution. Nevertheless, it mostly provides the reason for S17. The test pins 'because it is recent', so removing that
phrase without updating its semantic contract would fail a mechanical guard.

**Related:** S17 owns whole-piece integration. S1 determines relevance; S61's source-to-draft check should not promote a
recent fact merely because it appeared last.

**Recommendation:** Retain the recency safeguard in a compact cohesion paragraph; do not treat recent user corrections
as unimportant when they change the task.

**Principles:** Named failure; density; user steering.

## S17: Keep and narrow

> Integrate new information with the full context and restructure the whole piece when needed so its priorities,
> concepts, vocabulary, and flow remain cohesive before and after the addition.

**Assessment:** Integrating new information into the whole piece is a distinct behavior, not merely another wording
preference. 'Restructure the whole piece when needed' is sensible but offers no stop condition and can induce a full
rewrite after a trivial factual correction.

**Related:** S16 supplies the trap, S13 protects information during restructuring, and S61 verifies the edited whole.
The cohesion ownership test asserts several exact phrases.

**Recommendation:** Preserve the integration rule. Reorganize only as far as necessary to keep the requested outcome,
terms, and relationships coherent.

**Principles:** Change scope; density.
